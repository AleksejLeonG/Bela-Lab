## Advice and saved matches

Advice is for practice and analysis. Probabilities depend on the available cards and the simulated player model. Results may be uncertain and the app may contain errors. Use your judgment when relying on advice. Export or back up matches you wish to keep, especially before replacing or resetting the app.
