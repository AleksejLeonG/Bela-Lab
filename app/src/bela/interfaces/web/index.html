MANUAL TRACKING

Record a game.

  1. Set up the deal. Enter your starting hand, trump, dealer, caller and score.
  2. Record declarations. Enter spoken details and revealed cards, then confirm who won the meld contest.
  3. Track every move. Select the card played at the table. On your turn, you can also select a card from your hand.
  4. Correct and continue. History supports replacement, insertion and deletion. Every valid change is saved automatically.
