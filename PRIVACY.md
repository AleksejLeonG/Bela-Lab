# Bela Lab Privacy Policy

Applies to Bela Lab 1.0.0 for Windows. Last updated 25 November 2026.

## Data on your device

Bela Lab runs its game engine and analysis on your device. The desktop window communicates with the engine over a loopback connection on your computer. The app has no accounts, developer-operated cloud storage, advertising, or app analytics.

- **Match names, cards, scores, game history and bot checkpoints** let you save and resume games.
- **Analysis inputs, estimates and Review notes** let you reopen analysis and compare decisions.
- **Language, appearance, card preferences and setup drafts** restore your settings and unfinished setup.
- **Desktop startup logs and temporary runtime information** help diagnose startup problems and manage the local window.

The Windows app normally uses `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Bela Lab`. Browser dashboard preferences and drafts are stored in the browser profile you use. The app keeps local data until you remove it.

## Third-party software and sharing

Microsoft Edge WebView2 provides the Windows display. It includes Microsoft Defender SmartScreen and may send data to Microsoft under Microsoft's privacy practices. Windows diagnostic settings control optional diagnostic data. [WebView2 data and privacy](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/webview2/concepts/data-privacy) · [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement)

You decide whether to export or share anything. If you post on GitHub or use it to contact the maintainer, GitHub handles that activity under its own privacy statement. Public issues may be read by anyone. The maintainer uses information you choose to provide to respond and troubleshoot. [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

## Export and delete

Use **Library → Export** to save a copy of the active match. Before backing up or removing the entire data folder, close Bela Lab. Copy `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Bela Lab` for a backup, or delete it to remove local matches, review notes, analysis records, settings and logs. Uninstalling from Windows Settings or removing the portable program folder leaves this data intact. If you used the browser dashboard, clear its site data separately to remove its preferences and draft.

## Contact

For a privacy question or request about information shared with the maintainer, use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/AleksejLeonG/Bela-Lab/issues) to request a private contact channel. Include only the minimum information needed on the public issue.

Depending on the law that applies, you may have the right to access, correct or delete information you shared with the maintainer and to complain to your data protection authority.
